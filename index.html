A Central Illinois original

Built face to face, one batch at a time.

Millionaire’s Roast began through the Cass County CEO program, where founder Maximiliano Gomez turned a high school idea into a working coffee business.

Today, the brand is based in Springfield and still connected to Beardstown, growing through local markets, online orders, and a commitment to coffees people genuinely want to drink again.

Quick answers

Good coffee, straightforward answers.

Do you offer five-pound bags?

Yes. Five-pound bags start at $80, depending on origin. Most are $80; our premium Costa Rican Tarrazú Honey is $85. See the live shop for current availability.

How do I order?

Use our Square shop to browse current coffee and cacao products and check out securely. Choose whole bean or your preferred grind for coffee where available; the shop has the latest stock.

What coffee is available right now?

The lineup rotates as green coffee lots sell through. Visit the live shop for the current origins, roast levels, tasting notes, and formats.

Can I buy whole bean or ground coffee?

Yes. Whole bean and multiple grind options are offered where available. Choose your preference on the product page before adding it to your cart.

Where can I buy locally?

Visit The Cottage at 201 W Main St in Beardstown, or find us at seasonal Springfield markets. Our drinking chocolate is available at The Cottage and our market booths, subject to stock. Check Facebook or Instagram for our next market dates before heading out.

What makes the coffee specialty grade?

Specialty coffee starts with higher quality green coffee. We select lots for their origin, traceability, and flavor clarity, then roast them in small batches to bring those qualities forward.
