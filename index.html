Our story

A small roastery in Central Illinois.

Maximiliano Gomez started Millionaire’s Roast in high school through the Cass County CEO program. Today we roast coffee and cacao in small batches in Springfield, and sell at the markets and shops that connect us to Beardstown and our neighbors.

Our cacao comes from Hacienda Victoria.

Our classic drinking chocolate and nibs start with Nacional Arriba cacao from this estate on the Ecuadorian coast. We source it through CocoaSupply and roast it in Springfield. For coffee, we select specialty-grade lots for their origin, traceability and flavor.

Wholesale inquiries
How do I order and receive my purchase?

Our online checkout is on Square. Shipping, local pickup and local delivery depend on your items and address. Allow about 48 hours for preparation, plus shipping time.

How does the Discovery Box discount work?

Look inside the lid for a code that takes 10% off your first online purchase of bagged coffee. Enter it at checkout on your phone or computer.

Can I buy coffee by the five-pound bag?

Yes. Most five-pound bags are $80. Our Costa Rican Tarrazú Honey is $85. Square lists current origins, tasting notes and whole-bean or ground options.

Are the chocolate-covered espresso beans available?

They’re still in development. Our 52 g chocolate bars are available now at $4 each, tax included.
